# Creality K2

The K2 runs Klipper and Moonraker, so it gets [everything a Klipper printer gets](klipper.md), plus:

- **CFS units.** Each connected CFS (up to four) shows on the MMU / AMS card with its four slots, T1A to T4D.
- **Each slot's filament**: its colour and material.
- **What's loaded**: the active slot is marked.
- **Humidity and temperature** of each CFS unit.

Loading and unloading stay on the printer's own screen.
